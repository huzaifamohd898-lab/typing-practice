About Typing Mitra

Typing Mitra is a learning project for people who want to practice Hindi and English typing. Our goal is to make typing practice simple and accessible.

We are improving the website step by step. Always verify the requirements of your typing examination with its official notification.

Privacy

This basic version processes your typing test in your browser. It does not include an account system or save your test history online.
